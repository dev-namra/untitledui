Frequently asked questions

Can I change my plan later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account dashboard. Changes will be reflected on your next billing cycle.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied with your purchase for any reason.

Is there a free trial available?

Currently, we do not offer a free trial, but you can start with the Basic plan and upgrade as your needs grow.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. There are no contracts or hidden fees. You can cancel your subscription at any time.

How do I contact support?

Support is available via live chat and email for all plans. We provide priority support to Business and Enterprise customers.
